01 / About

Four connected
community units.

The community is an umbrella that connects a student AI Club, a research community, a policy committee, and an AI Division under the university IT department.

Shared purpose

Learning, careful practice, and questions that matter in a university setting.

Our responsibility

To test ideas honestly, protect people’s information, and keep human judgement in the loop.
