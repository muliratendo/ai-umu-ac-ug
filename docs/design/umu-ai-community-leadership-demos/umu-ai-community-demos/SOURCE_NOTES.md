# Source notes

## Revised proposal source

Primary source: [UMU-AI Community Proposal (Draft)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1x-sAueA0lyvQONqQCjJQvgoltE34Hmg7kLSsCU5Xbsk/edit?usp=sharing), revised working draft dated 17 November 2026 and described as draft for consultation and institutional approval. The full proposal summary supplied in the brief was therefore used as the controlling source. No missing facts were inferred.

## Functional references

The public [MIT Senior Leadership](https://orgchart.mit.edu/senior-leadership) and [MIT President](https://orgchart.mit.edu/leadership/president) pages were read for functional inspiration only: linked offices/officeholders, navigable organizational relationships, position descriptions, and directory-to-profile journeys. MIT branding, typography, colors, logos, imagery, wording, portraits, and distinctive composition were not copied.

## Visual source

The supplied UMU-AI `index.html` and `styles.css` and the existing static demo package remain the visual source: warm paper/cream, deep ink, teal, restrained orange/yellow, editorial type, thin borders, numbered groupings, proportionate whitespace, and field-note/orbit motifs.

## Changes from the older club-only concept

This revision replaces the prior club-only directory and its former four-unit leadership presentation. The current demo is driven by the revised proposal’s UMU-AI Community root, three governance groupings, three connected operational divisions, campus/faculty representation, technical project categories, supporting arrangements, and separate institutional relationships. Old club-only profile pages were removed from the package to avoid silently merging conflicting models. Unrelated Home, Community, account, and authentication demo pages were preserved.

The President profile now uses the revised supplied name “Mr. Mulira Tendo Mukisa”. The Speaker profile reflects the revised emphasis on orderly deliberation, member participation, procedural fairness, and authorised public speaking. Editorial functions are represented under the Social Media, Blog and Newsletter Division rather than being attributed solely to the Speaker.

## Status and unresolved interpretations

The package persistently discloses that the structure is proposed and pending consultation/institutional approval. “Named in draft” does not mean appointed. Optional roles are not treated as established. The proposal does not establish university-level research, financial, contractual, disciplinary, privacy, ethics, safeguarding, or system-administration authority for UMU-AI leaders.

Unresolved items include constitutional alignment and ratification; confirmed appointments, terms, eligibility, election/appointment routes, succession and handover; Board composition/chair/mandate; optional role establishment; campus-register discrepancies; the distinction between technical Project Leads and Project Team Coordinators; faculty representation scope; and editorial approval, privacy, safeguarding, copyright, accessibility, and publication responsibilities.
