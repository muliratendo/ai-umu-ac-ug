UMU-AI Community · revised proposal

A proposed structure for shared work.

This directory models the revised UMU-AI Community Proposal (Draft), dated 17 November 2026. It presents governance groupings, three connected operational divisions, representation, and institutional relationships without claiming that the structure is approved or that named appointments are current.
