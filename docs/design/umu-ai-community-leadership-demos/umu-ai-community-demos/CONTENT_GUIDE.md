# UMU-AI revised leadership content guide

This is an editorial handoff for a static design prototype. It is not an implemented CMS, approval workflow, permission system, governance arrangement, or claim that the revised proposal is institutionally approved.

## Persistent status language

Use exactly: **“Proposed UMU-AI structure — working draft for consultation and institutional approval. Names, appointments, terms, and mandates require confirmation.”** Distinguish `Named in draft — appointment to be confirmed`, `To be appointed`, `To be nominated`, `Subject to approval`, `Optional — establish if needed`, and `Mandate pending constitutional/institutional approval`.

## Entities and separation

Model separate entities for office/role, person, appointment/term, division, campus/faculty scope, governance body, body membership, typed organizational relationship, letter, and source/provenance record. An appointment links person + office + term + status. A letter stores author and issuing office at publication time so later leadership changes do not rewrite historical authorship.

## Required profile fields

Every office/body profile must include Overview, Reporting List, Biography, and Letters to the community as visible anchored sections. Every section needs source-grounded demo content, a missing-information placeholder, an expandable “Editorial reference: what to include” guide, role-specific guidance, and source/status notes.

### Overview

Mandatory fields: approved title and plain-language summary, purpose and intended benefit, scope, principal responsibilities, routine versus strategic work, decision authority and limits, what the office recommends/coordinates/approves/escalates, delegations and source, constitutional or terms-of-reference links, selection/election/appointment route, eligibility, appointing and ratifying body, term dates once verified, acting/absence/succession/removal/handover arrangements, work-plan references, constituents/services, approved contact/access arrangements, accountability deliverables, conflicts/confidentiality, accessibility/inclusion, source, content owner, verification date, and review schedule.

Role prompts include: President—coordination and representation do not confer university authority; Treasurer—financial reporting is distinct from unilateral spending approval; Patron/Board—oversight does not replace authorised university offices; Project Manager—coordination does not equal sole expertise or research approval; Speaker—deliberation, procedural fairness, and authorised public speaking; media roles—editorial verification, attribution, approval, corrections; representatives—constituency, participation, feedback, escalation; liaison—referral/review awareness, not legal or ethics approval.

### Reporting List

Store reports to/accountability, coordination/leadership, direct reports only when explicit, appointment/selection, advisory oversight, academic/technical supervision, collaboration/institutional liaison, constituency, and reporting outputs. Each edge needs source node, target node, relationship type, direction, scope, source passage, proposed/approved status, dates, cadence if approved, escalation route, delegation/limitations, verification owner/date. Use “Not specified in the proposal — confirmation required.” when absent.

### Biography

For named people, show the supplied name and role/status, then state that a verified biography has not been supplied. Collect approved display name, honorific if supplied, confirmed term, short/long biography, verified affiliations, education/qualifications, evidence-backed experience/work, contribution/interests, attributed priorities, portrait permissions/alt text/credit, official links, voluntary pronunciation/pronouns, consent, source/editor/approval/review dates, and previous holders/history. For unnamed roles use “Officeholder to be appointed; biography pending confirmation.” For bodies use “Body background and member biographies.”

### Letters to the community

Use the empty state: **“No approved letters have been supplied for this demonstration.”** Each sample must be labelled: **“Editorial layout example — not a published letter or a statement by the named officeholder.”** Capture title, summary, issuing office, author/co-signatories and roles at publication, audience, publication/effective dates, category/tags, context, accessible HTML, decisions/recommendations, actions/deadlines, sources/links, attachments and accessibility, publication authority/approval, contact route, translations, corrections/history, related messages, permalink/archive, privacy/safeguarding/copyright/accessibility, and retention owner. Never publish private complaints, confidential minutes, or casework.

## Revised proposal structure inventory

### Governance

UMU-AI Community root; General Assembly; Community Advisory and Oversight Board; Executive Committee. The proposal names President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, Speaker, Social Media Officer, Project Manager, and Campus Representatives in the Executive Committee. Do not convert this into an invented President-only chain of command.

### Operational divisions

Learning and Student Engagement, coordinated by the Vice President; Research and Projects Development, coordinated by the Project Manager; Social Media, Blog and Newsletter, led by the Social Media Officer. Optional/supporting roles include Training/Peer-Learning Coordinator, Blog and Newsletter Editor, Social Media/Community Engagement Team, Audio/Video/Design Contributors, Partnerships and Alumni Coordinator, Finance and Resource Mobilisation Committee, and Records, Compliance and Responsible-AI Liaison. Optional roles are not automatically elected seats.

### Representation and technical roles

Campus labels are Nkozi, Lubaga, Mbale, Ngetta, Mother Kevin–Nsambya, Fort Portal, and Masaka. Do not equate Gulu with Mother Kevin–Nsambya. Named proposed/previously nominated faculty representatives are Nkozi Science — Mr. Kato Shafiq and Nkozi Built Environment — Mr. Emmanuel Collins Mukenye. The seven proposed technical categories are Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Speech Processing, Robotics, Knowledge Representation and Reasoning, and Search, Planning and Decision-Making. Do not confuse technical Project Lead, Project Team Coordinator, and Campus Representative.

## Relationship register

`RELATIONSHIP_REGISTER.csv` records source node, target node, type, direction, source passage, approval status, uncertainty, and verification date. University offices are institutional relationships, not operational subordinates. Faculty mentors, external experts, and university staff are collaborators/supervisors, not subordinates of student Project Leads.

## Recommended publication states

`draft`, `under review`, `published`, and `archived` are proposed content states only. They do not exist as implemented permissions in this static package. Unresolved questions include constitutional ratification, appointments and terms, Board composition/mandate, optional role establishment, campus/faculty register, election and succession rules, and editorial approval/publication responsibilities.
