01 / About

Useful skills,
careful practice.

The club exists to help members move from interest to informed practice. We learn how AI systems work, try tools in small projects, and make time to discuss their limits, risks, and effects on people.

Our approach

Hands-on, peer-supported, and grounded in questions that matter in a university setting.

Our responsibility

To test ideas honestly, protect people’s information, and keep human judgement in the loop.
